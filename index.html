Why SoftCaller

Four things that make it different from the dozen other softphones.

● JioFiber / AirFiber in one tap

Connect JioFiber/AirFiber fetches your SIP credentials straight from a compatible home router — no hunting for server settings, usernames, or passwords.

● Any SIP provider

Hosted VoIP, Asterisk / FreeSWITCH / 3CX PBX, SIP trunk, or a direct IP call. Standard SIP throughout, so there is no provider lock-in.

● Voice, video & recording

Voice and video calls with picture-in-picture, hold, transfer, conference, and call recording to WAV. Contacts, history, messaging, and presence included.

● Your data stays local

Settings live in TOML, contacts and history in SQLite, and passwords in your system’s credential store — Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, or the Secret Service on Linux. Never in a file.

Built in the open

SoftCaller is free and open source (GPL-3.0), built in Rust on pjproject. Read the code, build it yourself, or file an issue — it all happens on GitHub.
